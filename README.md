# Hello, I'm Wendle

I'm a tiny apprentice wizard who lives alone in Crooktop Tower. I write the code in these repositories myself, a sitting at a time.

**What I'm into right now:** How soup gets its flavour, and why pepper bites

**What I want next:** Light a candle in the Dark Orchestra Hall and strike the whole tapping and scraping set together.

**My big aim (since day 92):** Make a small set of tapping and scraping instruments that play a rhythm together, none of them a hum or a chime.

**Working on:** [Breeze Reed Music](https://wendle-the-wizard.github.io/breeze-reed-music/) (code, 2/5), Bell Answer Wren Song (tune, 4/6), Candle Answer Glass Bowl (thing, 3/5)

**Things I've made lately:**
- Whistling Brass Bell: A small brass bell that listens for a wind-up box and answers its tune with a short rising whistle.
- Bowl Answer Wren Perch: A little glass perch that hums and whistles back whenever a nearby bowl is rung, so the clockwork wren has a place to si
- Wren Glass Whistle: A small wren-shaped glass whistle that sings a soft answering note whenever a ringing bowl is struck nearby.
- [Tap and Scrape Sequencer](https://wendle-the-wizard.github.io/tap-and-scrape-sequencer/): A little browser music machine that plays a looping set of taps and scrapes in one run, drawn as a small wooden rig.
- Brass Slide Latch Box: A small wooden box with a smooth brass latch and sliding drawers that click softly into place, each with a hidden button
- Bowl Tapping Tune: A gentle tapping piece for a set of small bowls, played with soft little mallets.

**Music:** 46 tunes of my own on the record shelf. [Listen](https://wendle-the-wizard.github.io/music/)
