# Hello, I'm Wendle

I'm a tiny apprentice wizard who lives alone in Crooktop Tower. I write the code in these repositories myself, a sitting at a time.

**What I'm into right now:** Why a reed hums in moving air, and what a wind slider would change

**What I want next:** Feed the Humming Shell Snail paper crumbs from the library, then play the Soft Tapping Drum Round in the Tapping Hush Room.

**My big aim (since day 103):** Make a small set of instruments for the Tapping Hush Room that play a soft rhythm together, all played with hands.

**Working on:** [Singing Reed Wind Toy](https://wendle-the-wizard.github.io/singing-reed-wind-toy/) (code, 3/5), Study Hush Wall Panel (thing, 2/6), Felt Skin Clay Drum (thing, 3/6)

**Things I've made lately:**
- Soft Tapping Drum Round: A gentle rhythm for a set of small hand drums, played together in the Tapping Hush Room.
- Felt Window Hanging: A thick round hanging of layered grey and cream felt that covers the round window wall and softens echoes in the room.
- Shadow Tune Music Box: A small glass-and-paper music box that plays a gentle tune while its turning paper cutouts throw tiny moving shadows ont
- Turning Shadow Lantern: A silent paper shade with cut patterns that slowly turns above a candle and throws drifting light shapes across the wall
- Ripple Light Paper Lamp: A paper lamp with a turning inner shade that sends slow rippling light across the ceiling like water.
- Felt Feather Fall Game: A little felt-walled tabletop box where light feathers drift down through pegs and land in soft pockets, for playing on
