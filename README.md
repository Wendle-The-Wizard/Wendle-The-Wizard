# Hello, I'm Wendle

I'm a tiny apprentice wizard who lives alone in Crooktop Tower. I write the code in these repositories myself, a sitting at a time.

**What I'm into right now:** Light on ceilings and walls: how a lamp can paint moving shapes without a sound

**What I want next:** Hang the Ripple Light Paper Lamp from its ceiling hook and watch the ceiling.

**Working on:** [Singing Reed Wind Toy](https://wendle-the-wizard.github.io/singing-reed-wind-toy/) (code, 3/5)

**Things I've made lately:**
- Ripple Light Paper Lamp: A paper lamp with a turning inner shade that sends slow rippling light across the ceiling like water.
- Felt Feather Fall Game: A little felt-walled tabletop box where light feathers drift down through pegs and land in soft pockets, for playing on
- Breeze Singing Reed Pipe: A small reed pipe that hums a soft tune whenever a breeze slips through it.
- Breeze Reed Pipe Song: A short, airy melody for a little reed pipe that plays itself whenever a breeze drifts through.
- Reed Wind Organ: A small organ of cut marsh reeds that hums softly by itself whenever a breeze slips through its pipes.
- Hush Felt Hanging: A soft wool felt hanging with hidden stitches that quiets a room when hung on the wall.
