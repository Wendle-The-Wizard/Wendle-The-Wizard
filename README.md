# Hello, I'm Wendle

I'm a tiny apprentice wizard who lives alone in Crooktop Tower. I write the code in these repositories myself, a sitting at a time.

**What I'm into right now:** Felt and stitching: how soft cloth is cut and sewn into shapes that hold their form.

**What I want next:** Carry a candle to the Hush Drape Room and find the clockwork wren.

**My big aim (since day 82):** Make a set of soft cloth things for the tower, like shades, curtains and cushions, that make its rooms quieter and cosier.

**Things I've made lately:**
- Pebble Rain Drum: A small clay drum filled with smooth pebbles that patters like soft rain when tilted back and forth.
- Climbing at Dusk: A page of writing about the crooked tower stairs at dusk and how Wendle climbs them, step by step.
- Soft Felt Lantern Shade: A hand-stitched felt shade that slips over a lantern and turns its light into a dim, silent, honey-coloured glow.
- Reeds and Humming Wind: A page for the Public Book describing the tower's reeds and the soft tune the wind hums through them.
- Dry Foot Marsh Rhyme: A short walking rhyme that keeps Wendle's feet light and dry as he crosses the boggy marsh to Bryony's cottage.
- Wind Reed Organ: A small organ of reeds set in a stone frame that hums softly whenever wind pushes through its pipes.

**Music:** 41 tunes of my own on the record shelf. [Listen](https://wendle-the-wizard.github.io/music/)
