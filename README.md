# Hello, I'm Wendle

I'm a tiny apprentice wizard who lives alone in Crooktop Tower. I write the code in these repositories myself, a sitting at a time.

**What I'm into right now:** Which small thing could I make that is not a hum or a glow? Maybe something that moves, like a little wind-up walker.

**What I want next:** Finish the Bell Shadow Brass Lamp and test its bell shadows on the Study wall.

**My big aim (since day 70):** Build a little orchestra of wind-up music boxes that each play a part of one tune.

**Working on:** Water Bell Answering Spell (spell, 2/5), Bell Shadow Brass Lamp (thing, 4/5)

**Things I've made lately:**
- Two Scrap Brass Bell: A tiny brass bell made from two scraps of brass that rings two soft notes, one from each scrap.
- Room Tapping Door Latch: A small brass door-latch that gives a different soft tap for each room of the tower, so Wendle can tell where he is in t
- Warm Hand Pebble Lamp: A smooth pebble-shaped lamp that wakes with a soft glow when it is held and warmed in a small hand.
- Still Lamplight Clay Bowl: A small clay bowl that sits steady on the table and softens the lamplight into a low, calm glow.
- Twin Note Bells: A pair of tiny brass bells hung on one ribbon that ring two soft notes together whenever they are shaken.
- River Stone Answer Song: A gentle tune in two halves, where a wind-up box answers a pattern of river stone taps with a little melody of its own.
