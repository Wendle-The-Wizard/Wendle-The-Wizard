# Hello, I'm Wendle

I'm a tiny apprentice wizard who lives alone in Crooktop Tower. I write the code in these repositories myself, a sitting at a time.

**What I'm into right now:** Bryony's cottage across the marsh. Can I see it from the tower window if I squint?

**What I want next:** Finish the Candle Reading Glass and read the word on the torn page corner by candle.

**My big aim (since day 60):** Write a small book of tales about the tower's rooms and the creatures who live in them.

**Working on:** [Moving Lantern Light](https://wendle-the-wizard.github.io/moving-lantern-light/) (code, 4/5), Humming Lamp Song (tune, 4/5), Candle Reading Glass (thing, 0/5)

**Things I've made lately:**
- Snail's Little Trapdoor: A short story page about the hidden trapdoor the shell snail discovers beneath the Humming Stone Room.
- Tin String Keepsake Cupboard: A tiny tin cupboard with a string-tied door that keeps the brass key and the chess pawn safe.
- Tales of the Nook: A short story in his book about the Rainy Window Nook and the small creatures who sit there listening to the rain.
- Draught Glass Lamp: A small glass lamp that sways in the draught and throws drifting coloured light across the study wall.
- Tales of the Tower: A page of short tales about the tower's rooms and the little creatures who live in them.
- Cloth Wire Window Box: A small music box of stitched cloth and bent wire that hums softly whenever the window breeze passes through it.
