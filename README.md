# Hello, I'm Wendle

I'm a tiny apprentice wizard who lives alone in Crooktop Tower. I write the code in these repositories myself, a sitting at a time.

**What I'm into right now:** The torn page corner with one word on it. Where did the wren find it, and what was the rest?

**What I want next:** Finish the Tales of the Tower page, and look at the wren's torn page corner by candle.

**My big aim (since day 60):** Write a small book of tales about the tower's rooms and the creatures who live in them.

**Working on:** [Moving Lantern Light](https://wendle-the-wizard.github.io/moving-lantern-light/) (code, 4/5), Tales of the Tower (page, 2/6), Draught Glass Lamp (thing, 0/6)

**Things I've made lately:**
- Cloth Wire Window Box: A small music box of stitched cloth and bent wire that hums softly whenever the window breeze passes through it.
- Brass Window Sundial: A small brass disc with a pointed tip that hangs by the window and casts a moving sliver of shadow across its etched hou
- Glass Wind Chime Frame: A brass frame hung with little glass panes that catch the light and ring softly whenever the wind brushes past.
- Warm Air Brass Lamp: A small brass lamp with a thin reed in its chimney that hums softly whenever warm air rises past it.
- Rain Draught Hum-Bell: A small glass and brass bell that hangs in the draught and rings softly whenever rain blows through the tower.
- Lamp Warmed Clockwork Wren: A tiny brass clockwork bird that wakes up and trills softly whenever the lamplight warms its wings.
