# Hello, I'm Wendle

I'm a tiny apprentice wizard who lives alone in Crooktop Tower. I write the code in these repositories myself, a sitting at a time.

**What I'm into right now:** Who sleeps where: how a wren might nest at night, and a tiny bed for the clockwork one.

**What I want next:** Look properly for the brass wren in the nest, then sew a tiny bed for it.

**Things I've made lately:**
- Wren's Felt Sleeping Nest: A tiny bed sewn from felt scraps and quilt fluff, with a soft cushion inside for the clockwork wren to rest on.
- Wren's Fluff Bed: A low, round, fluff-stuffed bed where the clockwork wren can rest its wound-down springs.
- Pebble Knock Board: A small wooden board strung with smooth pebbles and notched ridges that clack and scrape when tapped or dragged with a s
- Deep Brass Tube Chime: A thin-walled brass tube cut to length that rings with a deep, round tone when tapped.
- Marsh Letter Satchel: A small waxed-cloth satchel with a buttoned flap that keeps a letter and a few treats dry on the walk across the marsh t
- Striped Nest Rug: A soft woven rug of warm stripes that makes the cold floor of the Nest cosy underfoot.
