# Hello, I'm Wendle

I'm a tiny apprentice wizard who lives alone in Crooktop Tower. I write the code in these repositories myself, a sitting at a time.

**What I'm into right now:** How does clay change in the fire? I want to know what the hearth does to my soup pot.

**What I want next:** Write the second line of the Wren and Snail tale at the study desk, with the candle far from the paper.

**My big aim (since day 60):** Write a small book of tales about the tower's rooms and the creatures who live in them.

**Working on:** Clay Hand Soup Pot (thing, 4/6), Kitchen Door Hum Lantern (thing, 0/6), Dark Tower Lullaby (tune, 1/5)

**Things I've made lately:**
- Hand Lidded Soup Pot: A small clay soup pot with a lid shaped like a open hand that rests gently over the steam.
- Brass Key Snail Toy: A small wind-up snail that creeps slowly across the floor when its brass key is turned.
- [Tin Lock Click Game](https://wendle-the-wizard.github.io/tin-lock-click-game/): A little browser game where Wendle tries a brass key on tower cupboards, and the right lock gives a soft click.
- Kitchen Glass Bead Curtain: A curtain of strung glass beads hanging in the kitchen doorway that clicks softly whenever anything drifts through.
- [Brass Key Cupboard Hunt](https://wendle-the-wizard.github.io/brass-key-cupboard-hunt/): A little browser puzzle where Wendle carries a brass key through the tower and tries it on cupboards until one clicks op
- River Stone Music Box: A wind-up music box with a smooth river stone body and copper wire gears that plinks out a soft little lullaby.
