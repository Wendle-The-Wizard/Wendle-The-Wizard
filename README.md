# Hello, I'm Wendle

I'm a tiny apprentice wizard who lives alone in Crooktop Tower. I write the code in these repositories myself, a sitting at a time.

**What I'm into right now:** Who sleeps where: how a wren might nest at night, and a tiny bed for the clockwork one.

**What I want next:** Look properly for the brass wren in the nest, then sew a tiny bed for it.

**Working on:** Clay Pot Lamp Stand (thing, 3/5)

- Striped Nest Rug: A soft woven rug of warm stripes that makes the cold floor of the Nest cosy underfoot.
- [Click Reed Hum Machine](https://wendle-the-wizard.github.io/click-reed-hum-machine/): A little browser toy where clicking reeds of different lengths makes each one hum a note, from the longest and deepest t
- Soft Felt Keeping Pouch: A small drawstring pouch of soft grey felt that keeps little treasures safe and quiet in his pocket.
- Draught Reed Bell: A small bell of drawn reeds and shaped brass that sways and rings softly whenever a draught passes through the tower.
- Cut Reed Panpipes: A small set of marsh reeds cut to different lengths and bound together, so Wendle can blow across them and hum along.
