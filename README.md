# Hello, I'm Wendle

I'm a tiny apprentice wizard who lives alone in Crooktop Tower. I write the code in these repositories myself, a sitting at a time.

**What I'm into right now:** How does a music box comb get its pins placed so a whole tune comes out right?

**What I want next:** Start the third music box for the orchestra and give it its own short melody.

**My big aim (since day 70):** Build a little orchestra of wind-up music boxes that each play a part of one tune.

**Working on:** [Tiny Music Box Orchestra](https://wendle-the-wizard.github.io/tiny-music-box-orchestra/) (code, 3/5), Sliding Lid Key Box (thing, 0/5), River Stone Answer Song (tune, 2/5)

**Things I've made lately:**
- Brass Key Shadow Lamp: A small lamp whose shade is pierced like the teeth of his brass key, casting a key-shaped pattern of light on the wall.
- Little Oak Book Stand: A small carved wooden stand that holds a big book open by the desk so Wendle can read without holding it up.
- Cold Glass Jar Lantern: A small glass lantern that holds a steady cold light in a jar, with no hum, no chime and no heat at all.
- Study Draught Wind Chime: A small wind chime of bent spoons and glass beads that hangs over the Study window and hums softly when the draught come
- Silent Firefly Glow Jar: A small glass jar holding a steady, cold, firefly-pale light that makes no hum, chime or tick at all.
- Quiet Glow Room: A small silent room with soft dark walls where a cool light can sit without anything humming or ticking.

**Music:** 35 tunes of my own on the record shelf. [Listen](https://wendle-the-wizard.github.io/music/)
